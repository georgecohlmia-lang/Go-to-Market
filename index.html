STEP 01

Look at your best customers. Write down how they got to you.

Don’t start with the channel. Start with the group from the last session, the one the business really works for. Ask one question: how did they get to you? Where were they the week before they showed up?

Ask the people you interviewed and your test group where they’d go looking for something like yours.

STEP 02

Pick one channel. Write what you’ll do, how often, and who answers.

If they came because a professional they trust suggested you, your channel is that professional. If they came because they read something you wrote at eleven at night, your channel is your writing. One. Just one.

STEP 03

Pick the one number you’ll count, and the date you’ll judge it.

Decide how you’ll judge it before you start, or you’ll drop it on a feeling in about six weeks. One number, something people did, with a target on it.
